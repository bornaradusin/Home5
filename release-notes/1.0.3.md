### What's new

- **Your own certificates for HTTPS.** Home Assistant behind a reverse proxy or ingress with its own certificate authority now works over `https://`: put the authority's `.crt` file in `/data/homebrew/PPSA11019/assets/`, and Home5 trusts it alongside the console's and its own. Thanks to tonytwostep for this one.
- **Coming from 1.0.2:** your rooms and sign-in stay. If you add a `.crt` file, keep a copy: a clean reinstall replaces the app's folder.
