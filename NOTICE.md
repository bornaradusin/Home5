## What the app is built on

- **PS5 Native App Boilerplate**, `https://github.com/mpereiraesaa/ps5-native-app-boilerplate`,
  commit `9c0b994a048521af6fb84c73ded364504fe250e9`, Copyright (C) 2026 BlackBearReloaded,
  GPL-3.0-or-later. The app's start-up code, its runtime module (`sce_module/libc.prx`, a
  clean-room shim generated by the boilerplate) and the tools that link and sign
  `eboot.bin` are the boilerplate's. `src/h5_ps5.c` lays the frame out in display memory and
  opens the screen as the boilerplate's example does. The boilerplate's own pictures and
  sound are not used: `tools/build.sh` removes them.
- **PS5 payload SDK** v0.42, `https://github.com/ps5-payload-dev/sdk`, downloaded by the
  boilerplate and checked against its SHA-256: the headers and link stubs Home5 is
  compiled against. It is not part of the app. `tools/build.sh` adds one stub of its own,
  for `sceCommonDialogInitialize`, which the SDK lacks.
- **Mbed TLS** 3.6.7, `https://github.com/Mbed-TLS/mbedtls`, Apache-2.0 (or GPL-2.0-or-later),
  compiled into the app for HTTPS, with Home5's settings (`src/h5_mbedtls_config.h`).
- **Inter** (`fonts/`), Copyright (c) 2016 The Inter Project Authors, SIL Open Font
  License 1.1 (`fonts/OFL.txt`). The app's `assets/fonts.bin` is rendered from it by
  `tools/make-font.py`.
- **Mozilla's root certificates** (MPL-2.0), as the build computer's `ca-certificates`
  package has them, in the app's `assets/ca-certificates.crt`.

`PPSA11019-sources.tar.xz`, with every release, has Home5's source, the boilerplate at
its commit, the Mbed TLS release, and `PARTS.txt`, which says where the rest comes from.

## Home Assistant

Home5 talks to Home Assistant through its documented sign-in (`/auth/login_flow`,
`/auth/token`) and WebSocket API. No Home Assistant code, logo or artwork is in Home5; its
icon and backgrounds are its own (`art/make-art.py`).
